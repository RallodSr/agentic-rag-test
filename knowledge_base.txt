International Travel Policy: Employees traveling abroad for business must obtain written approval from their department head at least 14 days before departure. The company covers economy-class airfare, accommodation up to 4,500 THB per night, and a daily meal allowance of 1,800 THB. Travel insurance is mandatory and is arranged centrally by the HR department. Personal side trips may be combined with business travel, but all additional costs are the employee's responsibility.

Domestic Travel Policy: Business trips within Thailand require approval from the employee's direct supervisor at least 3 working days in advance. Employees may claim actual accommodation costs up to 2,000 THB per night and a daily meal allowance of 800 THB. Use of personal vehicles is reimbursed at 6 THB per kilometer, and receipts must be submitted within 15 days after the trip ends.

Remote Work Policy: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to team scheduling and manager approval. Fully remote arrangements are considered case by case and require director-level sign-off. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours from 10:00 to 16:00, keep their calendar up to date, and attend the mandatory all-hands meeting in the office on the first Monday of every month.

Annual Leave Policy: All full-time employees are entitled to 12 days of paid annual leave per year, increasing to 15 days after 3 years of continuous service. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for leave longer than 2 days. Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over to the next calendar year and must be used before the end of March.

Expense Reimbursement Policy: All business expenses must be supported by original receipts or official tax invoices. Claims are submitted through the finance portal and must be filed within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses above 10,000 THB require pre-approval from the department head. Reimbursements are paid together with the monthly payroll cycle.

IT Security Policy: Employees must use company-issued devices for accessing internal systems and enable multi-factor authentication on all corporate accounts. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days. Connecting to internal systems over public Wi-Fi requires the company VPN. Any suspected security incident must be reported to the IT Security team within 24 hours.
